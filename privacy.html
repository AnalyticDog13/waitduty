Privacy Policy

LXA Wait Duty · Last updated September 23, 2026

LXA Wait Duty is a small website that adds a chapter member's wait duty days to their own Google Calendar. This page explains what it does with your information.

What the site accesses

When you tap "Add to Google Calendar", you sign in with Google and grant permission to create calendar events (calendar.events). The site uses that permission only to create or update your wait duty reminder events in your primary calendar. It does not read, change, or delete any of your other events.

What the site stores

Nothing on a server. The site has no backend or database. It runs entirely in your browser and talks directly to Google Calendar. The Google access token stays in your browser's memory and is gone when you close the page. The name you pick is saved in your browser's local storage so the page remembers it next time. You can clear it by clearing site data.

Sharing

Your Google data is not sold, shared with anyone, or used for anything other than creating your wait duty events. The site's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can revoke the site's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Events already added stay in your calendar until you delete them.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue on the project's GitHub page.
