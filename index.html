LXA Wait Duty

Pick your name and add all your wait duty days to Google Calendar, with reminders at 2:00 PM and 6:30 PM on the day.

    About this app

    LXA Wait Duty helps members of our chapter remember their wait duty (cleaning a floor of the house on an assigned day). Pick your name, and the app adds each of your assigned days to your Google Calendar as two short reminder events at 2:00 PM and 6:30 PM, showing which floor you're on.

    To do that, it asks Google for permission to create events on calendars you own. It only creates or updates its own wait duty events. It never reads, changes, or deletes your other events, and nothing is stored on a server. See the privacy policy.
